# Low Strike website

**https://happzgirafe.github.io/** — the website of [Low Strike](https://github.com/HappzGirafe/DesertStrike), a free
tactical shooter: Terrorists against SWAT on five maps, against bots or online. Play it in the browser at
[happzgirafe.github.io/play](https://happzgirafe.github.io/play/) or download it for Windows, macOS, Linux and Android.

Don't edit this repository by hand: it is made by `Tools/publish_github_io.ps1` in
[HappzGirafe/DesertStrike](https://github.com/HappzGirafe/DesertStrike). The page comes from its `GitHubIO/` folder;
the browser version (`play/`), the video and most pictures (`media/`) and `online.json` come from its `docs/` folder.
