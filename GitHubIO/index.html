01 / The round

Buy. Fight. Plant. Defuse.

Terrorists have to blow up bomb site A or B. SWAT have to stop them. Every round is a fresh plan, and the money you earn decides what you carry into the next one.

  1. 01

    Buy

    0:25

    Press B in your own spawn. $800 to start, more for every win and kill. Sell guns back in the first 30 seconds.

  2. 02

    Fight

    1:55

    Under two minutes on the clock. Terrorists push a site, SWAT hold the map. One life per round.

  3. 03

    Plant

    3 s

    Hold E on site A or B to plant the C4. Once it is down, it explodes after 40 seconds.

  4. 04

    Defuse

    10 s / 5 s with kit

    SWAT have to get to the bomb in time. A $400 defuse kit halves the wait.

First to 8 rounds wins the match. You can set anything from 1 to 16 in the menu, with or without friendly fire.

04 / Modes

Ways to play

Against bots

From 1v1 up to 5v5 on Easy, Normal, Hard or Extreme. Bots buy weapons, plant the bomb and defuse it.

Offline · always works

Online rooms

Sign up with a nickname and a password, then create a room or join one. Up to 5v5 with no IP addresses or router settings; bots take the free places.

Accounts · friends · invites

LAN party

Host a game on your WiFi and friends on the same network join in. Hold V to talk with voice chat.

Downloaded game · WiFi · voice

In the browser

Nothing to install: bots on every map and online rooms with everyone. Log in for your skins and Tokens.

Chrome · Edge · Firefox · Safari

Skins & Tokens

Skins for every weapon: Gold, Arctic, Desert Camo, Urban Digital, glowing neon wraps and the karambit. Earn Tokens online and spend them in the shop.

Friends

One account on every computer and phone. See when friends are online and invite them to your room.

Your crosshair

Set it up like in CS2: color, length, thickness, gap, dot, outline and T shape.

Runs on anything

Low, Medium or High graphics, a 30/60/120 FPS limit and 3D resolution. Intel and Apple Silicon Macs, and touch controls on phones.
