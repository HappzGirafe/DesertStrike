Gameplay

One minute from a real match, recorded in the game.

What's in the game

Rounds of about two minutes: buy, fight, plant or defuse.

Bots

1v1 – 5v5

From 1v1 up to 5v5 against bots on Easy, Normal or Hard. They buy weapons, plant the bomb and defuse it.

The bomb

C4 · 40 s

Plant the C4 on site A or B in 3 seconds. It explodes after 40 seconds. SWAT defuse it in 10 seconds, or 5 with a kit.

Shop

$200 – $10000

13 weapons, from the Glock-18 to the RPG. Buy in your own spawn during the first 25 seconds of a round.

LAN games

Wi‑Fi · V

Host a game on one computer and join from another on the same WiFi. Bots take the free places. Hold V to talk.

Skins

Karambit

An inventory with skins for every weapon: Gold, Arctic, Desert Camo, Urban Digital and the karambit knife.

Runs on laptops

30 / 60 / 120 FPS

Graphics Low, Medium or High, a frame limit and 3D resolution. Works on Intel and Apple Silicon Macs.
