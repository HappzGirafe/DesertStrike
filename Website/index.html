Gameplay

Almost two minutes from a real match on the Village map, recorded in the game.

What's in the game

Rounds of about two minutes: buy, fight, plant or defuse.

Bots

1v1 – 5v5

From 1v1 up to 5v5 against bots on Easy, Normal, Hard or Extreme. They buy weapons, plant the bomb and defuse it.

The bomb

C4 · 40 s

Plant the C4 on site A or B in 3 seconds. It explodes after 40 seconds. SWAT defuse it in 10 seconds, or 5 with a kit.

Shop

$200 – $10000

14 weapons, from the Glock-18 to the RPG. Buy in your own spawn during the first 25 seconds of a round, and sell your guns back in the first 30.

Online and LAN

Wi‑Fi · V

Play online with anyone: create a room or join one, up to 5v5, with no IP addresses or router settings. Or host a game on your WiFi. Bots take the free places. Hold V to talk.

Skins

Karambit

An inventory with skins for every weapon: Gold, Arctic, Desert Camo, Urban Digital and the karambit knife.

Laptops and phones

30 / 60 / 120 FPS

Graphics Low, Medium or High, a frame limit and 3D resolution. Works on Intel and Apple Silicon Macs, and on Android phones with touch controls you can move.

Four maps

de_dune + 3

de_dune in the desert, Village, Halloween (a haunted village at night) and Industrial with a warehouse, containers and a three-room B site.

Accounts & friends

Friends · Halloween

Make an online account with a nickname and a password: it works on every computer and phone, keeps your skins and settings, and brings friends over the internet, who see when you are online and invite you to their rooms. Plus Halloween, the RPG Web skin and Extreme bots.

Your crosshair

1.17

Set the crosshair like in CS2: color, length, thickness, gap, dot, outline, T shape. A gun that rests for 3 seconds fires its next shot dead on.
