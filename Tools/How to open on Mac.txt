LOW STRIKE ON MAC - HOW TO OPEN IT THE FIRST TIME

macOS only opens apps silently when the developer pays Apple for a Developer ID.
Low Strike is a free hobby game, so the first time macOS says it "cannot be opened"
or that Apple "could not verify" it. Allow it once like this:

1. Double-click Low Strike. When the warning appears, click "Done" (or "OK").
2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security and scroll down to "Security".
3. Next to '"Low Strike" was blocked...', click "Open Anyway" and enter your Mac password.
4. Click "Open". From now on it opens normally.

(Tip: move Low Strike to your Applications folder first, then do the steps above.)

LAN games: the first time, macOS may ask whether Low Strike may accept incoming network
connections. Click "Allow" so other PCs on your WiFi can find and join your game.

Voice chat: the first time you hold V (or test the microphone in Settings), macOS asks
whether Low Strike may use the microphone. Click "Allow".
